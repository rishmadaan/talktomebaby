Hear your draft, word by word, where you wrote it.

TalkToMeBaby reads prose files aloud inside VS Code. Markdown, txt, rst, org, tex, adoc. A Reader panel opens beside your file. The sentence being read gets a band. The word being spoken lights up. Click any word to jump there.

Reading

Built so you always know where the voice is.

  • Sentence band, word sweep

    A band sits behind the sentence being read. The sweep moves across the words on top of it, one at a time. The same band appears in the source editor.

  • Jump by clicking

    Click any word in the Reader and playback moves there. From the source editor, press alt+j to jump to wherever your cursor is.

  • Speed from 0.5× to 2×

    Pitch stays put. Speed is applied locally, so changing it never re-synthesises audio. Presets for the common steps, a fine slider for the rest. Your setting is remembered.

  • A cache that saves credits

    Audio is kept on disk, 200 MB by default. Reading a file a second time costs no extra API credits.

Voices

Offline by default. Premium voices with your own key.

WhereVoiceWhat leaves your machine
macOSThe system say voice. The default. No setup.Nothing. Fully offline.
Windows, LinuxA free voice, ready out of the box. No API key.See PRIVACY.md for the details.
OptionalElevenLabs, OpenAI, or Sarvam AI for Indian languages. You bring your own key. Keys live in VS Code's secret storage.The text being read is sent to that provider.

Install

Two ways in. Both free.

  • Press ⌘P on macOS or CtrlP on Windows and Linux, paste the command, press Enter.
  • Open a Markdown or text file and run TalkToMeBaby: Read Document from the Command Palette.
ext install rishmadaan.talktomebaby
